Top three activities to do at Busan

Visit Magnate Café

A famous café connected to Jimin’s family, loved by ARMYs for its cozy atmosphere and BTS connection.

Explore Gamcheon Culture Village

Walk through colorful artistic streets and capture cinematic views that feel straight out of a BTS concept film.

Visit Gwangalli Beach at Night

Watch the illuminated Gwangan Bridge, enjoy street performances, and experience Busan’s iconic nighttime skyline beside the sea.
